Leave SUC Start Date on "None" to use each FIU's regular Yield/CMGR for the whole FY (as before). Pick a month to switch a SUC-configured FIU's revenue — from that month onward — to SUC Yield × expected Data Fetch volume instead, regardless of its regular billing model. The SUC period itself splits into two quarters: the first 3 months (SUC Cliff) grow at SUC Cliff CMGR, then every month after that (SUC Recovery) grows at SUC Recovery CMGR (falling back to the FIU's regular CMGR if that's left blank) — SUC Yield doesn't change between the two, only the growth rate does. FIUs missing SUC Cliff CMGR or SUC Yield, or with no usable DF count, stay on their regular Yield/CMGR the whole FY, and that's called out in the results rather than left silent. Months governed by SUC values are highlighted like this (hover a cell to see whether it's Cliff or Recovery), and seeded historical actuals (Apr–Jul) are highlighted like this, in the Annual tables.

10. DF Yield Analysis

DF Yield = Total Revenue ÷ Total DF Count for the scope and month shown. Revenue only counts months that are actually billed (same rule as everywhere else); DF Count includes every FIU that reported a DF count that month, regardless of billing model — the same broad population as the DF count tables above. That means DF Yield reflects revenue earned across the whole Data Fetch footprint, not just the FIUs currently billing on it, so it can differ noticeably from any single FIU's configured Yield. A scope with no DF volume that month shows "—" rather than a divide-by-zero.

Yield & CMGR config

Current yield and expected compound monthly growth rate (CMGR) per FIU, used for the current month and to project every remaining month of the FY. CMGR is a decimal — enter 0.05 for 5% monthly growth, or 0 for flat/no growth. SUC Cliff CMGR, SUC Recovery CMGR, and SUC Yield only come into effect once a SUC Start Date is set on the Monthly Revenue tab: SUC Cliff CMGR drives growth for the first 3 months of the SUC period, SUC Recovery CMGR drives growth for every SUC month after that (falls back to the regular CMGR above if left blank), and SUC Yield applies throughout the whole SUC period.

How revenue is computed — "Active Users"/"Unique Users" (same billing model) use the AU count as-is for the current month; "Data Fetch"/"Fix Billing" treat the DF count as a month-to-date total and project it to a full month using the as-of date (day-of-month ÷ days in that month). Every subsequent month in the FY grows that baseline usage figure by (1 + CMGR) compounded, with yield held constant. Revenue = usage × yield for every month. FIUs with a billing model that isn't recognized, or missing from a config, are shown separately rather than guessed.
